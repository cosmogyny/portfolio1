Aloha mai, I'm Caitlin.

When I was 10, I learned how to program by stealing source code, designing my friends' Xanga pages, and fixing things I broke.

As an adult, I've been helping local organizations and federal compliance environments with the technical side of things: websites, apps, databases, digital marketing.

I love applying creativity, leadership, and joy to the projects I'm part of. I'm really curious about the human-computer relationship, and how it shapes our world.

Since 2023, I've been somewhat self-taught and in directed learning. freeCodeCamp, Skillcrush, Harvard's CS50. I'm in a cybersecurity cohort and took a programming class or two (gotta make sure I love it), and want to get a post baccalaureate in computer science. Possibly a master's in cs, if the stars align.

Until then, I look forward to sharing what I've learned by helping local orgs with their technology while building upon a lifetime of learning. Feel free to get in touch with me about a project, idea, or job opening.
